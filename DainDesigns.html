    The studio

    A small studio with a big opinion.

    About

    DainDesigns is an independent creative studio. We design brand identities, ads, posters and clothing, and we run social media and marketing for the brands we build. We keep the team small so the people you brief are the people who do the work.

    We work with founders, makers and cultural organisations who would rather have one strong idea than ten safe ones. Projects start with a conversation and end with files you can actually use.

    The dot

    The dot is the full stop at the end of a decision.

    It sits in our logo, at the end of our emails and in the corner of every file we hand over. It means the work is settled and ready to leave the studio.

    How we work

    Four house rules

    • Decide early

      We bring a point of view to the first call and change it only when you give us a better reason.

    • Build the grid, break it once

      Every layout gets a system. One element per piece is allowed to ignore it.

    • Ship the small thing properly

      A business card or a loading screen gets the same care as the launch.

    • Leave the files clean

      Named layers, real fonts, documented colours. You should never need to call us to find a file.
